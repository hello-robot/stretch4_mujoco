# SG4 compliant fingers

`mjcf_generator.py` replaces the SG4's rigid fingers with this model whenever the URDF has the SG4 tool. In the model:

- the fingertips follow the path calibrated on robot 4026;
- the fingers stall against objects while the motor keeps turning, and that stretch sets the grip force;
- the steel strips bend and push objects.

| File | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `calibration_4026.py` | Fingertip calibration (`latest_model_planar.yaml`) as a dict |
| `sg4_spring_shapes.json` | Strip shapes from the render rig |
| `calibration.py` | Calibration to gripper-frame poses; strip fit |
| `compliant_fingers.py` | The MJCF edits |

## Joints, per finger

```
ctrl ─► position actuator ─► m ─[spring, FLEX_K]─► f ─[equalities]─► fingertip x, y, yaw
                                                     └─► band ─► strip hinges
```

| Joint | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `m` | `gripper_finger_<side>_joint`, driven by the actuator. 0 = cups meet, 0.5 = open, < 0 = squeeze; `pos_pct = 600·m`. |
| `f` | `sg4_<side>_follow`, pulled toward `m` by the `sg4_<side>_flex` tendon spring. |
| `sg4_<side>_tip_{x,y,yaw}` | Fingertip's planar joints, each tied to `f` by a cubic. |
| `sg4_<side>_band` | `f + BAND_GIVE·(m − f)`; strip hinges are quartics of it. |

`m`, `f` and the band are hinges on 1 g bodies with no geometry.

## Following the calibrated path

The three joint equalities `tip_k − tip_k₀ = a1·f + a2·f² + a3·f³` (k = x, y, yaw) leave the fingertip one degree of freedom, along the calibrated path. Each step, MuJoCo's constraint solver moves the fingertip joints whenever `f` moves. Contact forces on the cup travel back to `f` through the same constraints.

The `polycoef` values come from:

1. `tip_pose(side, pos_pct)`, which evaluates the calibration's u, v and θ splines and moves the result into the gripper frame (`CAMERA_IN_GRIPPER`). It then shifts the point `CUP_BASE` back to the cup's base.
2. `tip_polys()`, a least-squares cubic in `q = pos_pct/100` over 300 samples. The fit is within 0.1 mm.
3. `make_fingers_compliant()`. Since `q = 6f`, each coefficient `c_k` is multiplied by `6^k`. The constant `c0` becomes the body offset and the joint `ref`, so `qpos0` is the closed pose and `a0 = 0`.

## Free and blocked motion

- **Free:** `f ≈ m`, and the fingertip tracks the path. Opening the left finger to `ctrl = 0.25` settles at 226.8 mm, 57.0 mm, −62.2°, the calibrated pose for `pos_pct` 150.
- **Blocked:** the cup stops `f`, while `m` keeps going until `kp·(ctrl − m) = FLEX_K·(m − f)`. The grip torque is `FLEX_K·(m − f)`. Joint status reports `m`, as a real servo does.

Three `fingertip_compliance` hinges let the cup tilt flat onto what it touches.

## Strips

The URDF's rigid `gripper_finger_<side>_link` body and its meshes are removed. Two steel strips built from `sg4_spring_shapes.json` replace it, as both visual and collision geometry. The URDF's `gripper_fingertip_<side>_link` (cup, tag holder, ArUco tag) is kept and moved onto the x/y/yaw joints.

Each strip is a hinge chain: 12 × 13.9 mm segments for the outer strip, 14 × 7.7 mm for the inner. A `_draw` slide pulls unused length into the body. The segments are boxes sized from the file's `strip_t`, `shrink_t`, `band` and `shrink_dz`.

`spring_design()` fits the chains in three steps:

1. Map each rig frame onto the sim, from the body exit to the calibrated fingertip.
2. Fit the hinge angles to it with SLSQP.
3. Fit each angle with a quartic in `q`.

The result is cached in `autogenerated_sg4_spring_design.json`.

The first hinge and the draw slide are free. A `connect` pin closes the chain on the fingertip. Under load the band moves past the stalled `f`, so the strips bow outward. The strips collide with the world, but not with the gripper body or fingertips.

## Tuning

These values are estimates until they are measured on a robot:

- `FLEX_K`: grip force.
- `fingertip_compliance` in `defaults.xml`: cup tilt.
- `sg4_cups` friction: cup-on-cup grip.
- `BAND_GIVE`: strip bow.

To use another robot's calibration, pass its dict as `fingertip_calibration`. Bump `_SPRING_DESIGN_VERSION` after changing `_design_springs()`.
